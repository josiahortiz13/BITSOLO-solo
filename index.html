Got Questions

FAQ

Tap any question to expand the answer.

What is solo Bitcoin mining? ▾

Solo mining means your miner competes directly against the Bitcoin network to find the next block. If you find it, you keep the entire block reward — currently over 3 BTC. It's a lottery with real odds and real payouts.

Is BitSolo an authorized Canaan distributor? ▾

Yes. BitSolo (Ford Digital LLC DBA BitFord Digital) is an authorized U.S. distributor of Canaan Avalon miners. Every unit is authentic, warranted, and ships directly from US stock.

How hard is it to set up? ▾

Plug it in, connect to Wi-Fi, point it at your Bitcoin wallet address. That's it. No technical background required. If you can set up a router, you can run a miner.

How fast does it ship? ▾

Same-day shipping on orders placed before 2PM. We ship to all 50 states. No customs delays — everything ships from US soil.

What are the odds of winning a block? ▾

Odds vary based on your miner's hashrate vs. the total network. A solo miner recently hit Block 944306 and won $222,012 (3.128 BTC). It happens. The more miners you run, the better your odds.

How loud are the miners? ▾

Bitaxe units are near-silent — comparable to a laptop fan. Canaan Avalon units produce some fan noise but are far quieter than commercial ASICs. Most customers run them in a home office without issue.

What is the raffle? ▾

Every miner purchase earns you a raffle entry automatically. BitSolo runs periodic drawings where one winner takes home a prize package — no extra cost, no extra steps.

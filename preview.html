Got Questions

FAQ

What is solo Bitcoin mining? ▾

Solo mining means your miner competes directly against the Bitcoin network to find the next block. If you find it, you collect the full block reward — currently 3.125 BTC + network fees.

Are you an authorized Canaan distributor? ▾

Yes. We are an authorized U.S. distributor of Canaan Avalon miners. Every unit is authentic, warranted, and ships from US stock.

How hard is setup? ▾

Plug it in, connect to Wi-Fi, enter your Bitcoin wallet address. Done in under 15 minutes. No technical background required.

How fast does it ship? ▾

Same-day shipping on orders placed before 2PM. We ship to all 50 states from US soil — no customs delays.

What is flare gas Bitcoin mining? ▾

Oilfield flare gas is energy that would otherwise be burned off and wasted. We capture it and use it to power Bitcoin miners — turning waste energy into Bitcoin. We consult oil operators on how to implement this.

How loud are the miners? ▾

Bitaxe units are near-silent — like a laptop fan. Canaan Nano and Mini units produce some fan noise but are far quieter than commercial ASICs. Most customers run them at home without issue.

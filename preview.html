FAQ

What is solo Bitcoin mining? ▾

Solo mining means your miner competes directly against the Bitcoin network to find the next block. If you find it, you collect the full block reward — currently 3.125 BTC + network fees. It's a real lottery with real payouts.

Is BitSolo an authorized Canaan distributor? ▾

Yes. BitSolo (Ford Digital LLC DBA BitFord Digital) is an authorized U.S. distributor of Canaan Avalon miners. Every unit is authentic, warranted, and ships from US stock.

How hard is setup? ▾

Plug it in, connect to Wi-Fi, enter your Bitcoin wallet address. Done. Takes under 15 minutes. No technical background required — if you can set up a router, you can run a miner.

How fast does it ship? ▾

Same-day shipping on orders placed before 2PM. We ship to all 50 states from US soil — no customs delays.

What is the block reward? ▾

The current block reward is 3.125 BTC plus network fees. A solo miner found Block 944306 in April 2026 and collected 3.128 BTC — over $222,000 at the time.

How loud are the miners? ▾

Bitaxe units are near-silent — comparable to a laptop fan. Canaan Nano and Mini units produce some fan noise but are far quieter than commercial data center ASICs. Most customers run them at home without issue.
